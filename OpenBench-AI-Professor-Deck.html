Open with the practical question: when several LLMs can answer the same prompt, how do we choose the best one for a real product?

The Course Question

Open models challenge monopoly. Closed models concentrate power.

My project is not only a benchmark. It is a transparency tool.

OpenBench AI compares model behavior, cost, speed, safety, and quality so users are not forced to trust brand reputation alone.

Presentation thesis

  • Closed models often have stronger infrastructure, polish, and reliability.
  • Open models create competition, auditability, customization, and independence.
  • The risk is monopoly: if a few closed providers dominate, access, pricing, and rules become centralized.
  • OpenBench AI shows a practical way to compare models instead of accepting one winner by default.

The Problem

Model choice is technical, economic, and political.

Quality is hard to see

A polished answer can still be incomplete, unsafe, biased, or wrong.

Power is concentrated

Closed providers can control pricing, access, moderation rules, and model behavior.

Open models need proof

Open alternatives are valuable, but users need evidence that they are good enough for the job.

OpenBench AI turns the open-vs-closed debate from opinion into measurable comparison.

Open vs Closed Models

The tradeoff is not simple good vs bad.

Open models

  • Weights or architecture may be available for inspection or deployment.
  • Support customization, local hosting, research, and independence.
  • Can reduce monopoly pressure by giving users alternatives.
  • May require more engineering work to deploy safely and efficiently.

Closed models

  • Usually accessed through hosted APIs controlled by the provider.
  • Often strong in reliability, tooling, moderation, and product polish.
  • Can concentrate pricing power, access control, and data dependence.
  • Harder to audit because internals and training details are limited.

Monopoly Pressure

When one model ecosystem dominates, everyone builds around its rules.

The monopoly issue is not only who has the smartest model. It is who controls the platform: APIs, prices, rate limits, safety policies, developer tools, and access to frontier capability.

User Workflow

From question to recommendation in one run.

1. PromptUser enters a task or chooses a preset.
2. StreamAll configured models generate in parallel.
3. MeasureLatency, tokens, throughput, and cost are captured.
4. JudgeOutputs are anonymized and scored by rubric.
5. DecideThe app produces a leaderboard and decision brief.

Technical Highlight

Server-Sent Events make the benchmark feel alive.

Instead of waiting for every model to finish, the app streams start, token, complete, error, and done events to the browser. That makes latency visible and lets users compare generation behavior in real time.

Overall Ranking

Arena score blends intelligence with practicality.

A model should not win only because it sounds good. OpenBench combines judge quality with measured latency, cost, token efficiency, safety, completeness, and instruction following.

Weighted signals

  • Quality: judge-normalized score
  • Reliability: low hallucination risk
  • Fairness: bias resistance
  • Execution: speed, cost, and token efficiency
  • Usefulness: completeness and instruction following
